Training guide

School ERP
Step-by-step screens

How parents, students and teachers use the school app on a phone and the website, with a picture of every screen.

Before you start

Use this guide in a class. Each page has a picture of the screen and numbered steps. Follow the steps on your own phone or computer while the trainer shows them.

What is inside

Part 1: ParentsMobile app (8 screens) and website (7 pages)
Part 2: StudentsMobile app (9 screens) and website (5 pages)
Part 3: TeachersMobile app (7 screens) and website (8 pages)

Two ways in

  • The mobile app signs in with your mobile number (or email) and password. The app finds your school by itself.
  • The website signs in with your email or username and password, at the address your school gives you.
  • Both use the same password. Change it once and the other accepts it a moment later.

Good habits

  • Never share your password. Sign out on a phone or computer that is not yours.
  • If the mobile number on your record is wrong, the app cannot find you. Ask the school office to correct it.
  • If something looks wrong, note the page name and tell the school office.

The pictures use demonstration data: a sample school, a student called Aarav Sharma and a class teacher called Neha Verma. Your school's names and numbers will differ.

Part 1

Parents

For a parent or guardian: follow your child's attendance, fees, marks and hostel, ask for leave and talk to the school.

A. On the mobile app

Sign in to the app

  1. Open the School ERP app on your phone.
  2. Type your mobile number (or your email) in the first box. Type the number as you like: with or without +91 or spaces.
  3. Type your password in the second box.
  4. Press Sign in. The app finds your school by itself and opens your home screen.
  5. If the app shows Choose an account, tap the school and role you want.
  6. If the school gave you a temporary password, the app asks you to choose your own. Use at least 8 characters.

Wrong password too many times? The app waits 15 minutes. Do not keep trying; ask the school office.

Your home screen

  1. The blue bar shows your name. Use the magnifier to find a tile, the bell for notices and the arrow to sign out.
  2. Frequently Used holds the four tiles you open most.
  3. The Attendance ring shows the child's attendance. Swipe the card sideways for Payment Due.
  4. If you have more than one child, tap the child's name to change child.
  5. Scroll down for the sections: Academic, Exam, Fees, Hostel, Library, Administration, Other services.
  6. The bottom bar has Home, Time Table, Ask Query and Profile.

See attendance

  1. On Home, tap Attendance (under Academic).
  2. The boxes at the top count the days present, late and absent this month.
  3. Use Earlier and Later to move to another month. Each day is listed with its status.
  4. The ring on the Home screen shows the percentage. If a day looks wrong, talk to the class teacher.

Fees and payments

  1. Tap Fees & payments (under Fees).
  2. You see what was billed, what is paid and what is still to pay, with each receipt below.
  3. Payment is made at the school office; show the receipt number if there is a question.

Marks and results

  1. Tap Exams & results (under Exam).
  2. Each exam shows total, percentage, grade and rank.
  3. Incomplete means some subjects have no marks yet.
  4. Tap an exam to see every subject.

Ask for leave for your child

  1. Tap Student leave (under Academic).
  2. Choose the leave type, then type the dates as YYYY-MM-DD (for example 2026-10-30).
  3. Write the reason and press Send request.
  4. The class teacher decides. The decision shows in the Requests list below.
  5. A request that is still pending can be withdrawn.

Leave cannot be requested for dates that are already over.

Hostel: room and warden

  1. Under Hostel tap Room & warden to see the hostel, room, bed, meal plan and the warden's phone number.
  2. Tap Mess menu for the weekly menu: breakfast, lunch, snacks and dinner.
  3. Night roll call and Visitors show attendance at night and who visited.
  4. A day scholar sees "Not a boarder".

Notices and asking the school

  1. Tap Notices (under Other services) to read announcements from the school.
  2. Tap Ask Query in the bottom bar to send feedback or a complaint, and to read the replies.
  3. Press the button on that page to write a new one: choose the kind, the topic and write your message.

Profile, switching account and signing out

  1. Tap Profile in the bottom bar.
  2. Your name and school show at the top. Use Change password to choose a new password.
  3. Use Switch account if someone else needs to sign in on this phone, or if you have accounts at two schools.
  4. Always sign out on a phone that is not yours.

B. On the website

Sign in on the website

  1. Open the school website address in your browser.
  2. Type your email or username and your password, then press the sign-in button.
  3. Parents and students land on the portal. Teachers land on the dashboard.
  4. Use the sun/moon button at the top right to change between light and dark if your eyes tire.

The dashboard

  1. After signing in you land on Parent portal. The menu on the left is called My Children.
  2. Use the Child box to choose a child if you have more than one.
  3. The three cards show attendance for the last 30 days, fees outstanding and the class teacher with phone and email.
  4. Coming up at school and Notices from school are below.
  5. Turn on sends notices to this device as notifications.

Attendance

  1. Click Attendance in the left menu.
  2. Read the days and the percentage for the child chosen at the top.

Exams and results

  1. Click Exams & Results.
  2. Each exam shows the total, percentage, grade, rank and result.
  3. Press Report card to see marks by subject.

Fees and payments

  1. Click Fees & Payments.
  2. See what is billed, paid and balance, and each receipt.
  3. Payment is made at the school office.

Apply for leave

  1. Click Apply for Leave.
  2. Choose the type and dates, write a reason and send.
  3. The class teacher decides; you see the decision on the same page.

Certificates and documents

  1. Click Certificates & Documents.
  2. Ask for a bonafide, character, fee or transfer certificate.
  3. The page shows when the office has decided.

Feedback and grievance

  1. Click Feedback & Grievance.
  2. Write your message, choose feedback or grievance and send.
  3. The school's reply shows on this page.
Part 2

Students

For a student: see your timetable, homework, marks, attendance and fees, and ask for leave.

A. On the mobile app

Sign in to the app

  1. Open the School ERP app on your phone.
  2. Type your mobile number (or your email) in the first box. Type the number as you like: with or without +91 or spaces.
  3. Type your password in the second box.
  4. Press Sign in. The app finds your school by itself and opens your home screen.
  5. If the app shows Choose an account, tap the school and role you want.
  6. If the school gave you a temporary password, the app asks you to choose your own. Use at least 8 characters.

Wrong password too many times? The app waits 15 minutes. Do not keep trying; ask the school office.

Your home screen

  1. The blue bar shows your name. Use the magnifier to find a tile and the bell for notices.
  2. Frequently Used holds the four tiles you open most.
  3. The cards show your attendance and any payment due.
  4. Scroll down for Academic, Exam, Fees, Hostel, Library, Administration and Other services.
  5. The bottom bar has Home, Time Table, Ask Query and Profile.

Your timetable

  1. Tap Time Table in the bottom bar (or Timetable under Academic).
  2. Scroll down: each day of the week is listed with its periods and subjects, Period 1 first.

Homework

  1. Tap Homework under Academic.
  2. Each item shows the subject and the due date.
  3. Submitted means your teacher has marked it as handed in; Overdue means the date has passed.

Your attendance

  1. Tap Attendance under Academic.
  2. See each day of the month as present, absent or late.

Marks and results

  1. Tap Exams & results under Exam.
  2. You see total, percentage, grade and rank for each exam.
  3. Tap an exam to see every subject. You cannot change any mark.

Fees

  1. Tap Fees & payments under Fees.
  2. You can see what is billed, paid and due. Payment is made at the school office.

Apply for leave

  1. Tap Student leave under Academic.
  2. Choose the type, type the dates as YYYY-MM-DD, write the reason and press Send request.
  3. Your class teacher decides. You can withdraw a request that is still pending.

Leave cannot be requested for dates that are already over.

Hostel (boarders only)

  1. Under Hostel tap Room & warden for your room, bed and the warden's phone.
  2. Mess menu, Night roll call and Visitors are in the same section.

Profile and signing out

  1. Tap Profile in the bottom bar.
  2. Use Change password to choose a new password. Never tell it to anyone.
  3. Use Switch account or sign out when you finish on a shared phone.

B. On the website

Sign in on the website

  1. Open the school website address in your browser.
  2. Type your email or username and your password, then press the sign-in button.
  3. Parents and students land on the portal. Teachers land on the dashboard.
  4. Use the sun/moon button at the top right to change between light and dark if your eyes tire.

The dashboard

  1. After signing in you land on the Student portal. The menu on the left lists your pages.
  2. The cards show your attendance, fees and class teacher.
  3. Click any item in the menu to open it.

Timetable

  1. Click Class Timetable.
  2. Read the week: each period with its subject and teacher.

Homework

  1. Click Homework & Assignments.
  2. See what is set, the subject and the due date.

Exams and results

  1. Click Exams & Results.
  2. Each exam shows total, percentage, grade and rank. Press Report card for the subjects.
Part 3

Teachers

For a teacher: mark attendance, enter marks, set homework and decide leave requests, on the phone in the classroom or on the website.

A. On the mobile app

Sign in to the app

  1. Open the School ERP app on your phone.
  2. Type your mobile number (or your email) in the first box. Type the number as you like: with or without +91 or spaces.
  3. Type your password in the second box.
  4. Press Sign in. The app finds your school by itself and opens your home screen.
  5. If the app shows Choose an account, tap the school and role you want.
  6. If the school gave you a temporary password, the app asks you to choose your own. Use at least 8 characters.

Wrong password too many times? The app waits 15 minutes. Do not keep trying; ask the school office.

Your home screen

  1. Frequently Used shows My Class, Mark Attendance, Enter Marks and Students.
  2. Today's Summary shows how many students are present, absent and not yet marked today.
  3. Below are the groups of tiles you may open: Academics, Examinations, Transport, Hostel, Library and more.
  4. The bottom bar has Home, My class, Attendance and Profile.

My class today

  1. Tap My Class.
  2. Choose your class at the top if you teach more than one.
  3. The tiles show students, present, absent and not marked.
  4. The roll shows each student and the guardian's name and phone. Tap a status to call the guardian.

Mark attendance

  1. Tap Mark Attendance, then choose the class.
  2. Use Day before and Day after to change the day.
  3. Tap All present to start with everyone present.
  4. Tap a student to change the status: present, absent, late.
  5. Press Save attendance. Changing a past day asks for a reason.

Enter marks

  1. Tap Enter Marks. The list shows each exam with its class and maximum marks.
  2. Tap the exam, then choose the subject.
  3. Type each student's mark and press Save marks.
  4. A subject with theory and practical marks is entered on the website.

Students and leave

  1. Tap Students to open a group of pages: Students, Student summary, Health records, Behaviour and Alumni.
  2. Tap Attendance and leave for Mark attendance, My class today and Student leave.
  3. In Student leave, open a request and choose Approve or Reject.

Profile and signing out

  1. Tap Profile to change your password or switch account.
  2. Sign out on a shared phone.

B. On the website

Sign in on the website

  1. Open the school website address in your browser.
  2. Type your email or username and your password, then press the sign-in button.
  3. Parents and students land on the portal. Teachers land on the dashboard.
  4. Use the sun/moon button at the top right to change between light and dark if your eyes tire.

The dashboard

  1. After signing in you see the Dashboard. The left menu groups pages by area: Students, Academics, Timetable, Attendance, Examinations and more.
  2. Use the search box at the top (or Ctrl+K) to find any page or student.
  3. Click a menu group to open it, then click the page.

My class

  1. Click Dashboard → My Class.
  2. See your class, today's attendance and who has leave.

Daily attendance

  1. Click Dashboard → Today's Attendance (or Attendance in the left menu).
  2. Choose the date and the class.
  3. Click Mark unmarked present to start with everyone present, then change the exceptions in the Attendance status column.
  4. Add a note if you need to, and press Save attendance at the top right.
  5. The four cards at the top update: present, absent, late and class attendance.

Exams and marks

  1. Click Examinations → Exams.
  2. Open the exam and choose Marks. Type each student's mark and save.
  3. Results, ranks and report cards are made from these marks.

Homework

  1. Click Academics → Homework.
  2. Add a new homework: class, subject, title, description and due date.
  3. Open an item to mark who has submitted it.

Student leave

  1. Open Student Leaves.
  2. Read the request and the dates, then choose Approve or Reject.
  3. Approved leave shows on the attendance page.

Your timetable

  1. Open Teacher Timetable to see your periods for the week.

Notices

  1. Open Notices to read what the school has announced to staff and families.
